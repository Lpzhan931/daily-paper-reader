## 摘要
数值执行路径可以改变贪心输出；仅凭不匹配并不能预测剩余轨迹。在受测的 B570 栈上，我们使用已公开的发现数据选择一个无草稿、仅目标模型、微批大小为 1 的预测器，然后在保留的实时推测之前冻结完整的生成 ID 预测。它在 24 个重复稳定的提示上匹配 72/72 条有上限序列，包括四个参考发散提示的全部 12 次运行；同集参考匹配 60/72。该预测器对于此路径是充分的，但它既不能恢复资格认定，也不能识别出唯一的数值原因。单独的 B570 同状态分叉测试干预效应；RTX 提议/重放程序在共享数值例程的同时测试复现。我们单独评估成本。新近获得直接 ID 资格的 RTX 串行化在其插桩后的预填充后边界内更慢（目标/串行化延迟的 0.646×）。在固定的测得进展和目标工作下，即使去除草稿和残差成本，358H 仍低于持平。合格的 M4 Guard 优于仅目标，但输给固定推测。这些结果为所测路径上的无损兼容性和完整成本提供了可检验的诊断和工程决策；它们没有确立任何可移植的解码或控制增益。

## Abstract
Numerical execution paths can change greedy output; a mismatch alone does not predict the remaining trajectory. On the tested B570 stack, we select a draft-free target-only micro-batch-one predictor using exposed discovery data, then freeze complete generated-ID predictions before reserved live speculation. It matches 72/72 capped sequences on 24 repeat-stable prompts, including all 12 runs of four reference-divergent prompts; the same-set reference matches 60/72. The predictor is sufficient for this path, but it neither restores qualification nor identifies a unique numerical cause. Separate B570 same-state forks test intervention effects; RTX proposal/replay programs test reproduction while sharing numerical routines. We assess cost separately. Newly direct-ID-qualified RTX serialization is slower within its instrumented post-prefill boundary ($0.646\times$ target/serialized latency). At fixed measured progress and target work, 358H remains below parity even with draft and residual costs removed. Qualified M4 Guard beats target-only but loses to fixed speculation. These results give testable diagnoses and engineering decisions about lossless compatibility and complete cost on the measured paths; they establish no portable decoding or control gain.
